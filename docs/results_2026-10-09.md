# Results as of 2026-10-09

There are two studies of the GP plate-error correction, plus a follow-up that extends the first to a full plate. The results page (https://claude.ai/artifact/Bo7nC9EJkEKgZiy9FqVveq, v3) gives the formulas and a walkthrough of every figure. This file records what ran, from which code, and the main numbers.

Skill throughout means `1 − Σ(pred − truth)² / Σ truth²` on wells the method was not fit on, so 0 is "subtract the control median" and 1 is perfect.

## Study 1: all-control plates (the sweep)

Every well on these plates holds the same thing, so the true error is measured at every well. For each layout, a method is fit on *n* control wells and scored on the others.

**Data (55 surfaces):**
- all-negative plate 1: 19 timepoints, additive
- all-positive plate: 24 timepoints, max OD and OD at 24 h; relative
- 2026-06-04 gas/shaker plates: 5 distinct grids; relative, also shown additive

Plate 2 of the all-negative set was dropped as contaminated, and the blue-dye reads were excluded.

**Design:** n ∈ {3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40}; 8 placement settings × 30 layouts; 264,000 GP fits, each scored alongside median-only, nearest control, inverse-distance and row + column.

**Code:** the scoring code is identical to commit `a5c6d85`. The run started 2026-10-09 01:22 (`results/RUN_INFO.txt`, `RUN_DIFF_20261009T012242.patch`). Edits made while it ran: the run-record helper (`write_run_info`), the 384-well reader, and extra columns in `analyze.jl`. None of them touch scoring, and the analysis ran afterwards at 01:43.

### Placement (`results/fig_exp1_placement`)

- The mean skill of all 8 settings lands within a few hundredths, and within the chance difference between two batches of the same recipe (see the walkthrough notes).
- At 12 controls, spreading the controls out (minisum*) narrows the scatter across layouts; row/column balance (LHS) does not.
- Gas relative at 40 controls: two layouts with a control on a dead well score −6.3 and −7.2 and pull their settings' means down.

### Uncertainty (`results/fig_exp2_coverage`, `fig_exp2_sd_vs_error`)

- **Coverage:** with per-well noise included, the 95% range holds 0.67–0.70 of wells at 3 controls and 0.90–0.93 at 40, on every plate. The September version left the noise out and reported 0.34–0.79.
- **Does the GP's sd rank layouts?** No. Rank correlation of mean predictive sd with actual RMSE at 12 controls: −0.62 negative, −0.59 positive, −0.09 gas additive, −0.73 gas relative (dead-well artifact). The narrowest ranges come from overfit fits.
- **Lengthscale on a bound:** at the 2-well floor in 66% of negative, 71% of positive and 35% of gas fits, and at the 6-well ceiling in a further 8%, 5% and 13%.
- **Noise collapsing to about 0** (the GP copies its controls exactly): 16% of negative/positive fits and 30% of gas fits.

## Follow-up: full plate, 3–96 controls (`results/full_n/`)

The sweep stops at 40 because PlateArrays places both control classes. This run places only the scored class, uniformly at random: 30 layouts per n, all 96 leave-one-out layouts at n = 95, and the single layout at 96. That's 157,135 GP fits on the same 55 surfaces. Skill is pooled over layouts within each surface. Code: commit `62042b4`; log: `logs/full_n_2026-10-09.log` (26 min, 112 threads, 0 failed fits).

| | Negative | Positive | Gas relative | Gas additive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GP held-out skill, mean over n = 85–95 | 0.45 | 0.31 | 0.75 | 0.83 |
| GP leave-one-out skill at n = 95 | 0.49 | 0.35 | 0.81 | 0.85 |
| GP in-sample skill at n = 96 | 0.87 | 0.69 | 0.98 | 0.94 |

- The GP levels off well below 1. Part of each well's deviation is scatter that its neighbours can't predict.
- At 96 controls nothing is held out, so only in-sample skill exists. There the GP stays below 1 because of its noise term, while nearest control and inverse-distance reach 1 by copying.
- The dashed "ceiling" is not a bound. The GP passes it on the gas plates from about 47 controls (additive) or 65 (relative).
- On the all-negative plate, row + column beats the GP from about 28 controls. There the 2-well lengthscale floor binds in every fit, and relaxing it gives a modest leave-one-out gain (+0.03 to +0.08).

## Study 2: poster plate E4 (`results/poster_e4/`)

E4 is the 2021 poster plate: 384 wells, 146 reads every 10 min, glucose 0–5 g/L. Its 24 no-glucose and 24 top-glucose wells train a two-pass correction: negatives with additive error, then positives with relative error. The 336 middle wells (49 levels × 6–7 replicates) are never used for fitting. The score is **replicate skill**: the share of non-glucose variation in the middle wells that a correction removes. It is 0 for any shift or rescaling.

Code: commit `84df312`. Run: 2026-10-09 01:47 (`RUN_INFO.txt`, `RUN_DIFF_20261009T014730.patch`), 3,510 fits in 6 s.

**Reproduction:** all 26 checks against the 2021 notebook's printed output pass, at read 90 with the old per-axis (ARD) kernel. OD values agree to 1.7×10⁻¹³ and fitted parameters to 7×10⁻¹². The notebook's data file matches the raw reader export at all 384 wells × 146 reads.

| Method (all 24 + 24 controls) | Replicate skill, read 90 | Replicate skill, max OD | η², read 90 | Centre excess, read 90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uncorrected | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.74 | 0.29 |
| `subtract_and_normalize` | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.74 | 0.29 |
| **GP, one lengthscale** | **0.62** | **0.59** | **0.90** | **0.06** |
| GP, poster kernel | 0.59 | 0.60 | 0.89 | 0.05 |
| Inverse-distance | 0.49 | 0.50 | 0.87 | 0.16 |
| Nearest control | 0.27 | 0.18 | 0.81 | 0.17 |
| Row + column | 0.21 | 0.17 | 0.79 | 0.19 |

- **Centre vs edge:** uncorrected, the residual climbs from about −0.05 control windows at the edge to +0.37 at the centre. Both GPs flatten it to within about 0.05, and inverse-distance to about 0.15.
- **Controls needed** (pre-set threshold: 90% of the one-lengthscale GP's skill with all 24): both GPs reach it at **20 per class** for both measurements, and the other methods never do. At read 90 the one-lengthscale GP scores 0.01, 0.25, 0.43, 0.52, 0.58 and 0.62 at 3, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24 per class. Inverse-distance is better at 3–6, and the GP pulls ahead from about 8–12. The curve is still rising at 24.
- **Poster kernel fit:** its column lengthscale sits at the 6-well upper bound in both passes, and its row lengthscale is 2–3.5 wells.
- **Timing:** 52 of 384 wells (14%) peak at read 140 or later, so they are probably still growing at 24 h. Another 12 peak in the first 20 reads (flat wells).

## Caveats

- **The poster plate's negatives are not clean.** At read 90, interior no-glucose wells average 0.23 OD against 0.10 at the edge. The central excess is +0.14 OD in no-glucose wells but only +0.04 OD in top-glucose wells. All four 2021 plates (E1–E4) show the central block.
- **Lengthscales sit on their bounds,** so kernel comparisons partly reflect the chosen bounds.
- **Gas plates on the relative scale** are dominated by 2–8 wells per plate that barely grew (relative error near −12, about 90% of the squared error).
- **One plate per type,** so plate-to-plate reproducibility can't be measured. Gas plate 1 and plate 3 may be the same physical plate.
- **The 2021 notebook** mixed read 100 (inline cells) with read 90 (package cells and the poster figure). The reproduction uses read 90.
- **Coverage** is still below 95% with few controls.

## Open questions

1. Wells that barely grew, on the relative scale: exclude them from controls and scoring, keep the gas plates additive, or cap relative error?
2. Is the poster plate's central excess additive or proportional? Parked: Vasu decided on 2026-10-09 not to test it yet.
3. Lengthscale bounds: should the lower bound go below 2 wells? On the negative plate, the full-plate run suggests a small gain.
4. Are there other plates where the method was used? They would give the plate-to-plate replication that none of these results can show.
5. Next direction: calibration, extending n, or reframing the placement experiment.
